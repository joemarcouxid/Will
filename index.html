How to read this

  • Hard day Go all-out, rest fully between reps. Never two in a row.
  • Easy day Stay relaxed. You should finish feeling better than you started.
  • Track or Gym Pick one each day. The suggested one is marked, but either works.
  • 4 × 20 m 4 sprints of 20 meters.
  • 3 sets of 8 Do 8 reps, rest, three times.
  • Leave 2 in the tank Stop when you could still do 2 more good reps. Pick a weight where that's true.
  • Rest 4 min Really rest the full time. Short rest on sprints makes you slower, not tougher.
  • i Tap it on any exercise for how to do it.
  • Time it Write your time in the box. The app keeps your best.
  • Missed a day? Open the workout you want and tap "Make this today's workout". The rest of the week shifts so two hard days never land back to back.
  • Hurts? Pain (not just tired) means stop that exercise and tell your coach or a parent.
