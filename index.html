Speed + Hypertrophy Plan

How to use / key rules
  • Enter the contest date. The plan counts back from it to the plan start (defaults to this week): up to a 6-week hypertrophy block (shortened if time is tight) → 3 weeks strength/power → taper week → contest week. Extra time before the block is "Base".
  • Did a session on a different day? Use the Day menu on the card (or drag it on a computer). Changes are saved on this device only.
  • Red = high day (sprints, plyos, legs). Blue = low day (easy tempo, upper body). Avoid high days back-to-back; the page warns you.
  • Sprints come first, at full effort, with full rest (about 1 min per 10 m). If the flying 20 m is slower than usual, cut the lifting, not the rest.
  • RIR = reps in reserve (reps left before failure). Big lifts stay at 2–3 RIR and stop when the bar slows. Isolation work goes closer to failure.
  • Sharp or localized hamstring pain: no flys or sled; slope starts or tempo only, and get it checked.
  • Eat enough protein (~1.6 g/kg/day) and enough food to gain ~0.25–0.5% body weight per week; sleep 8–9+ hours.
