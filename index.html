Speed + Hypertrophy Plan

Rules
  • Red = hard day, blue = easy day. No two red days in a row.
  • Sprints first. All-out, full rest. Slower than usual? Cut the lifting, not the rest.
  • "2 left" = stop when you could still do 2 more reps.
  • Did it on another day? Change the Day menu.
  • Sharp hamstring pain? Stop sprinting and tell a coach.
  • Eat protein every meal. Sleep 8–9 hours.
